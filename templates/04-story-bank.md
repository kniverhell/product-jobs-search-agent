# Interview Story Bank

Stories accumulate here as you evaluate roles and prep for interviews. A story drafted for one role is
available for every future interview. The bank is the compounding asset — the more interviews logged,
the harder you become to surprise.

## How to add a story

Use this format. Tag each story with the dimensions it addresses (evidence match, leadership,
delivery, technical judgment, conflict, failure, etc.) so the `interview` mode can retrieve
the right ones quickly.

---

## Template

### Story: [Title — what happened, not what you learned]

**Tags:** [evidence-match] [leadership] [delivery] [technical] [conflict] [failure] [regulatory] [scale]

**Situation:** [1–2 sentences. The context, the stakes, the constraint.]

**Task:** [What you were responsible for specifically.]

**Action:** [What you did. Be specific about your role vs. the team's. Use first person, past tense.]

**Result:** [Numbers where you have them. Outcomes that are defensible in a room.]

**Reflection:** [What you'd do differently, or what this taught you — only include if it's genuinely interesting, not as filler.]

**Where it fits:** [JD language or question types this story answers well.]
