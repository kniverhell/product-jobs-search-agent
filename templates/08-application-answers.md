# Standard Application Answers

ATS systems ask the same questions repeatedly. Write your answers once here so they stay
consistent across applications. The `tailor` and `email` modes read this file.

**Facts live in `application-profile.md`, not here.** Name, contact details, links, work authorization,
sponsorship, relocation and other knockout answers have one home: that file (template in
`templates/application-profile.md`), which `fill` mode reads through `application_profile.py`. This file
keeps the prose answers.

Fill these out at the start of your search. Update when something changes.

## Compensation

```
Current / most recent base salary: [or "Prefer not to disclose"]
Desired base salary: [your target, not your floor — see config.md for floor]
Total compensation (most recent): [or "Prefer not to disclose"]
```

**Note:** Many ATS systems ask for current comp before you have an offer. You are not required
to disclose it. "Prefer not to disclose" or "I'd prefer to discuss after learning more about
the role" is a reasonable answer. See the `negotiate` mode.

## Work history (standard ATS fields)

For each recent role, have ready:
- Start and end dates (month/year)
- Employment type (full-time / part-time / contract)
- Reason for leaving (keep it neutral and short)
- Supervisor name and contact (decide in advance whether you'll provide this)

## Standard short-answer questions

### Why are you interested in this role / company?

[Write a 2–3 sentence template answer you can customize quickly. Focus on the specific
type of company and problem, not a specific company — that's the part you'll swap in.
e.g. "I'm drawn to companies where [your domain] is a core product problem, not infrastructure.
The kind of work that [specific thing you do] is where I've had the most impact."]

### What's your management experience?

[If you manage people: describe the team size, structure, and how long. Be specific.
If you don't manage people currently: describe cross-functional influence and any direct
reports you've had.]

### Describe your experience with [your domain].

[A 3–4 sentence answer that leads with the most recent and most relevant. This will be
customized per role, but having a baseline version saves time.]

### What's your biggest professional accomplishment?

[Pick one. Lead with the number, then the mechanism. Keep it to 3–4 sentences.
Rotate between your top 2–3 stories depending on which fits the role best.]

### Where do you see yourself in 5 years?

[Optional to answer specifically. A good generic answer: focus on the type of work and impact
you want to have, not on titles. Titles will change; the kind of work you want to do is real.]

## References

Have three ready: one manager, one cross-functional peer, one report or junior colleague.

| Name | Title (when you worked together) | Company | Relationship | Contact |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Name] | [Title] | [Company] | [Manager / Peer / Report] | [Email / phone] |

**Brief each reference before you give their contact information.** Tell them the role,
the company, and the two or three things you'd want them to emphasize. A reference who
isn't expecting the call is less useful than one who is.
