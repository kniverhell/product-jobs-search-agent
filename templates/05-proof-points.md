# Proof Points — Evidence Ledger

Every number or claim that goes into any external document must appear here first.

**Status key:**
- **Solid** — stated on the resume, defensible with specifics in a room
- **Derived** — arithmetic from stated figures; never present as if the resume says it directly
- **Soft** — true but approximate or hard to source; fine for general use, be ready to hedge
- **Open** — the claim exists but the number is missing; mark as `[TK]` in the resume

**Note on confidential figures:** If a number belongs to a former employer and is not public,
cite it directionally ("multi-million-dollar reduction") rather than specifically. The mechanism
is what you can defend; the exact figure is what you can't disclose. That distinction is a
positive signal in regulated industries — it shows you know what's proprietary.
