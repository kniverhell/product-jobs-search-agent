# Network — Who You Actually Know

The `contact` mode reads this file before drafting any outreach. It is only as good as what's here.

Fill this out before you start outreach. A weak tie beats a cold one every time, and you probably
know more people than you think.

---

## By former employer

For each company you've worked at, list the colleagues you'd actually reach out to.
Focus on people who could (a) refer you directly, (b) tell you about open roles you haven't seen,
or (c) give you an honest picture of what's happening at a target company.

## Instructions for `contact` mode

Before drafting any outreach note, check this file for:
1. Anyone at the target company (current employer section)
2. Anyone from a former employer who might know someone there
3. Any vendor contact who sells to the target company

A warm introduction that takes 30 minutes to arrange will outperform a cold application every time.
