# Needs evaluation

Rows with no score (blank or `TBD`) **and a decision still open**: status `New` or `Evaluated`, or a live
process (`Screening`, `Interviewing`, `Final`, `Offer`). Each needs a real evaluation (`modes/evaluate.md`)
before it gets a score. Remove a row when its score is written.

Applied, closed and passed roles keep a blank score and stay off this list. An applied role comes back on if
it moves to `Screening`.

Scores come only from evaluations, never from the Sheet (`SKILL.md` → *No human scores*). A row created from
the Sheet starts with a blank score and lands here if its decision is open.
