# Mode: Cover Letter

Only when the application asks for one, or the role is 4.5+ and worth the extra shot.

Read the evaluation report — block E is the whole letter. If block E is thin, go back and do the company research properly before writing.

## Structure — under 250 words

1. **The hook.** Something specific and true about *their* problem. Not "I'm excited about your mission." Something like: their onboarding flow still requires document upload for every applicant, or they're visibly hiring a fraud team, or they just entered a market with a regulatory regime you've built for.
2. **The bridge.** The one story from your experience that maps directly onto that problem. One story, told with its number, not a list of three.
3. **The asymmetry.** Why you're an unusual candidate for this specific company — not a generic strengths list. Pick the one asymmetry that fits *them*.
4. **The close.** Direct, no hedging, no "I would welcome the opportunity."

## Rules

- No adjectives about yourself. "Passionate," "results-driven," "proven track record" — none of it. Show, don't claim.
- Never restate the resume. The letter earns its place by saying something the resume can't.
- Enforce all standing constraints in `06-proof-points.md`.
- If there's nothing specific and true to say about the company, the letter shouldn't exist. Say that instead of writing filler.
