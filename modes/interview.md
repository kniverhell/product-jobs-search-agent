# Mode: Interview Prep

1. Read the evaluation report, the deep research if it exists, and `05-story-bank.md`.
2. **Map their likely questions to your existing stories.** Most of the work is retrieval, not invention — that's why the bank exists.
3. Identify the two or three questions where your stories are weak or missing. Draft skeletons, ask the user to fill the detail, then commit them to the bank so the next interview is easier.
4. Prep the specific questions this role will ask:
   - The exposure list from block B of the evaluation. Every gap will be probed. Have an honest bridge for each.
   - Short-tenure questions, if relevant to the candidate's history.
   - The company's actual domain problem, from the deep research. You should have a point of view, not just questions.
5. Five questions for the candidate to ask, drawn from the research. Not "what's the culture like."

After the interview, ask what came up that you weren't ready for, and put it in the bank. **The bank is the compounding asset in this whole system** — the more interviews logged, the harder you become to surprise.
