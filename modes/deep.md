# Mode: Deep — Company Research

Run before a first interview, or when a role scores 4.5+ and you want to go in hard.

Write to `reports/YYYY-MM-DD-company-slug-deep.md`. Six axes:

1. **Domain posture** — What does their actual product surface look like in your domain? Sign up if it's free and observe. What vendors do they appear to run? What's visibly strong or broken? This is your native lens and most candidates interviewing won't have done this.
2. **Recent moves** — Funding, launches, new markets, leadership changes, layoffs, incidents. Anything in the last 12 months that changes what this role is *for*.
3. **Regulatory exposure** — Which regimes, which jurisdictions, any public enforcement or consent orders. Where you have relevant experience.
4. **The team** — Who's in product leadership, where they came from, how long they've been there. Rapid turnover in the org you'd join is a real signal.
5. **The problem behind the req** — Why does this role exist *now*? Growth pressure, a product event, a compliance finding, a platform rebuild, backfill. The answer changes the entire interview.
6. **Your angle** — Given all of the above, the single thesis you walk in with. One sentence you could say in the first five minutes that reframes you from candidate to person-who-gets-it.

End with the five questions you should ask them — questions that only someone who'd done this research could ask.
