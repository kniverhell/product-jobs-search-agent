# Mode: Application Email

Formal email to a recruiter, a referrer, or a direct application address. **Draft only. Never send, never submit, never click.**

Produce:
- **Subject line** — role title, your name, and one differentiator if it fits. Boring and scannable beats clever.
- **Body** — under 200 words. Who you are in one line, why this role in two, the strongest single proof point with its number, the ask.
- **Attachment checklist** — which tailored resume file, cover letter if applicable, anything else the posting requested.
- **Signature block** — from the master resume header.

Three variants when the situation is ambiguous: cold application, recruiter reply, referral request. Label them so you can pick.

Save to the role's file in `tailored/`. Tell the user it's ready and let them send it.
